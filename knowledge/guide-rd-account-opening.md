# Staff Guide: Opening and editing an RD account

## Open a new RD account

1. Open Masters > RD Account > RD A/c Opening. The next free Account No is filled in for you.
2. Choose the member (search, or type the member number). The name fields fill in.
3. Enter the Deposit Date, the monthly Installment Amount, the Interest Rate and the Deposit Period (in months or years).
4. Maturity Date and Maturity Amount are calculated for you from those values. You can still edit them by hand; your edit stays until you change the amount, rate, period, unit or opening balance again.
5. Optionally enter an Opening Balance, nominee details, switch "Recovery Through Demand" on, and add special instructions.
6. Click Save. A confirmation box summarises the account and the form is ready for the next entry with a new Account No.

Only one nominee is stored. If you add a second nominee, the screen warns you that only the first is saved.

## Edit an existing RD account (Pass RD A/C)

1. Open Masters > RD Account > Pass RD A/C.
2. Choose the member. Their RD accounts appear in the Account No list.
3. Pick the account. Its current data fills the form.
4. Change what you need (amount, rate, period, maturity fields, special instructions) and click Save.
5. After saving, the form reloads the same account so you can see what was really saved.

## Good to know

- RD accounts and FD accounts share the same numbering, so the Account No is always taken from the system; do not type your own.
- RD has no GL head of its own. Money for an RD shortfall on a Regular Loan is credited to the Compulsory Deposit head (L1004).
- If saving an edit or deleting an account says the account does not exist, the Account No is wrong or the account was already removed; reopen the member and pick the account again.
