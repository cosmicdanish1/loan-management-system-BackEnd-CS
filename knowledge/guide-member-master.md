# Staff Guide: Member Master (adding and editing a member)

## Add a new member

1. Open Masters > Member Master.
2. Leave Member No blank. The system gives the next member number by itself when you save.
3. Fill in the form. First Name and Division/RO are the only fields that must be filled; everything else is checked only if you enter it.
4. Fill Personal and Employment (gender, date of birth, which gives the age by itself, designation, pay, insurance), Address and Status, and Organization and Nominee as needed. Insurance Amount can be entered only when "Insured" is ticked.
5. In Documents and Media you can add a profile photo, ID front and back, and a signature (draw it with the mouse or upload a JPG/PNG). They are held on the screen and uploaded together when you save the member.
6. Click Create. A success card shows what was saved. If something is wrong, an error box tells you which field to correct.

## Change an existing member

1. Open Masters > Member Master and click Search. Pick the member in the search box (by name or number). All details, saved photos, the signature and the KYC document list load.
2. Change what you need and click Update.
3. The Active/Inactive switch in the header row makes the member active or inactive. Reset clears the form and does not change anything already saved.

## KYC documents (Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID and so on)

A KYC document can be added only after the member has been saved, because it needs the member number. Save the member first, then add the documents. They can be viewed and deleted later.

## Checks the system makes

- Name, PAN, Aadhaar and mobile number formats, numeric ranges and dates are checked.
- Duplicates are refused: PF No, Aadhaar, PAN, FRS No, phone and e-mail must each be unique across all members.
- A member cannot be deleted if he or she has any loan, savings, FD, RD or surety records. This protects those records.

## If something goes wrong

- "Duplicate" type messages: another member already has that PF No, Aadhaar, PAN, FRS No, phone or e-mail. Search for that member before creating a new one.
- Cannot add a KYC document: save the member first.
- Cannot delete a member: they still have loans, deposits or surety records, so close or transfer those first.
- A "Database Error" with no clear reason: note what you entered and raise a query in Support Desk.
